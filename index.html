About Our Founder

The Vision, The Calling, and The Founder

Pastor Isaac Omonaiye is the founder and visionary leader of Living Spring Global Ministries (Rehoboth Chapel). Called with a clear apostolic mandate to herald the uncompromised Word of God, Pastor Isaac has dedicated his life to equipping believers, restoring hope to families, and building a global community anchored in faith, prayer, and divine purpose.

Under his visionary leadership, Living Spring Global Ministries was established on the revelation of Genesis 26:22 — "He named it Rehoboth, saying, 'Now the Lord has given us room and we will flourish in the land.'" With a deep passion for spiritual revival and kingdom expansion, Pastor Isaac envisioned a sanctuary where people from every walk of life could encounter the living presence of God, experience spiritual deliverance, and grow into maturity in Christ.

From its chapel in Brandon, Manitoba, to its expanding global outreach, Pastor Isaac Omonaiye leads the congregation with intense fervor for prayer, insightful biblical teaching, and a compassionate shepherd's heart. His ministry emphasizes:

  • ✦
    Apostolic Word & Discipleship: Teaching believers to walk in spiritual victory and sound doctrine.
  • ✦
    Fervent Prayer & Revival: Spearheading transformative prayer gatherings, including the monthly Breakthrough Vigil.
  • ✦
    Community & Global Impact: Fostering family fellowship, youth mentorship, and benevolent community care across Brandon and beyond.

Through his unwavering dedication to the Great Commission, Pastor Isaac continues to inspire thousands to surrender to Jesus Christ, walk in their divine identity, and build lives that glorify God.
